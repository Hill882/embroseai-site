## Why start with an audit

Most small businesses I talk to know AI could help, but not where to start or what's hype. Buying tools first usually means paying for subscriptions nobody uses. The audit flips that order: we look at the actual work first (the emails, the data entry, the follow-ups, the copy-and-paste between systems) and only then decide what, if anything, is worth automating.

Sometimes the honest answer is "not much yet," and that's a useful answer too.

## How it works

1. **Free 30-minute intro call.** We talk through how your business runs and where the time goes. If an audit isn't the right fit, I'll say so.
2. **Workflow review.** I sit with you and the people who do the work, in person within about an hour of Franklin or over video, and map the repetitive computer tasks step by step: who does them, how often, in which tools.
3. **Opportunity ranking.** I rank each opportunity by how much time it could free up, how hard it is to build, and how much risk it carries (customer-facing messages and money get extra care).
4. **Written roadmap and walkthrough.** You get the roadmap in writing, and we go through it together so you can decide what to do next: build it with me, build it yourself, or do nothing.

**How much does an AI audit cost?**
At Embrose AI the AI Audit is $1,500, fixed price. That covers the workflow review, the ranked list of opportunities, tool recommendations and the written roadmap.

**How long does the AI Audit take?**
It depends on how many people and workflows are involved. We set the timeline on the free intro call, before you commit to anything.

**Do you have to come on-site?**
No. I do audits in person within about an hour of Franklin, including Greenwood, Indianapolis and Johnson County, or fully remote over video.

No. The roadmap is yours. You can build it with me, with someone else, or on your own.

Then the roadmap will say so. I'd rather tell you that than sell you automations you don't need.

Usually I just need to watch how the work is done. If we look inside a system, I ask for the minimum access needed and document it.
