## What a Crew bot does

A Crew bot is an AI assistant trained on a written playbook for one job your team does on a computer. It reads what comes in, does the routine steps, and stops to ask a person whenever a decision matters.

Jobs businesses commonly start with:

- **Matching payments to invoices.** The bot reads new deposits, finds the matching open invoice, checks amount, customer and invoice number, and asks your team to approve marking it paid.
- **Preparing invoices** when a job closes, queued for approval before anything goes out.
- **Following up on late invoices.** It drafts a polite reminder; a person decides whether to send it.
- **Answering billing questions.** It drafts answers from your own records for your team to send.
- **Inbox triage and reply drafts** for the questions you answer every week.
- **Meeting notes and follow-ups**, written up with the follow-up emails and tasks drafted.

You pick the workflow that eats your week. Payment matching is a popular first step, but it doesn't have to be the first one.

## What stays human

- **Your team approves** refunds, credits, write-offs and every customer message.
- **Your accounts, your data.** The Crew runs in tools you own, with the least access needed.
- **Full activity log.** Every action is recorded, and your team can pause any bot at any time.
- **Nothing replaced overnight.** The Crew works alongside the people who manage your finances today. This is back-office help, not accounting or tax services.

It's an AI assistant set up to do one repetitive computer job, like matching payments or drafting invoice reminders, following a written playbook. At Embrose AI a person on your team approves anything sensitive.

No. Customer messages always wait for a person on your team to approve them.

No. The Crew handles repetitive back-office tasks such as invoice prep, payment matching and follow-ups. It works alongside whoever manages your finances today and doesn't replace them.
