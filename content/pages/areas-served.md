## Why in person matters for this work

AI setup goes better when I can sit next to the person who does the work and watch how it's really done: which screens they open, what they copy where, what they check before they hit send. That's hard to see on a video call. So for businesses close to Franklin I'd rather come to you, at your office, shop or job trailer.
