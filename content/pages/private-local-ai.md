## Why run AI locally

Popular AI tools send what you type to the provider's servers. For a lot of work that's fine. For some businesses it isn't: client documents, contracts, drawings, project files or anything you've promised to keep private. A local model runs on hardware you control, on your network, so the files and the questions you ask it stay there.

## What it's good for

- Asking questions about your own documents and procedures ("how do we handle X?")
- Summarizing and drafting from client documents without sending them out
- Working with drawings and project files that shouldn't leave the building
- Teams that want AI available to staff without a separate cloud account per person

## Honest trade-offs

- Local models are often smaller than the biggest cloud models, so some tasks work better in the cloud. Part of the audit is telling you which.
- Capable hardware costs money up front, and someone has to keep it running. I'll lay out the costs before you decide.
- It isn't the right fit for every business. The audit tells us honestly whether it makes sense for yours.
