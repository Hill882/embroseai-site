Privacy policy

Last updated: [go-live date]

Who runs this site

This website, embroseai.com, is run by Embrose AI LLC ("Embrose AI", "we" or "us"), based in Franklin, Indiana. If you have a question about this policy, email hello@embroseai.com or call or text (317) 777-1512.

The short version

  • We only get personal information that you choose to send us: through the contact form, by email, by phone or text.
  • We use it to reply to you and to talk about working together.
  • We don't sell or rent your information, and we don't use it for advertising.
  • The site has no analytics, no advertising trackers and no tracking cookies.

What you send us

The contact form. The form on the home page sends what you type (your name, phone, email, business name and message, whether you'd like an intro call, and any jobs you picked in the "What can we automate?" tool) to FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a form-delivery service. FormSubmit emails it to hello@embroseai.com. FormSubmit handles that data under its own privacy policy.

Email. Messages you send to hello@embroseai.com are delivered by our email providers to the inbox we use to read and answer them.

Phone calls and texts. The call and text links open your own phone app. Calls and texts go through phone carriers like any other call or text. The website doesn't see or record them.

How we use it

  • To reply to you, set up the intro call and talk about your business.
  • To prepare a quote and, if you hire us, to do the work and keep records of it.

We use service providers, such as our email, calendar and AI assistant tools, to help receive, organize and answer messages. They get only what they need for that job.

What the website itself collects

  • No analytics or tracking. The site doesn't use analytics, advertising pixels or tracking scripts.
  • No cookies. The site doesn't set cookies.
  • Self-hosted files. Fonts, images and scripts load from embroseai.com itself, not from outside font or script services.
  • Hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical details such as your IP address when you visit, for security and to keep the service running. GitHub's privacy statement covers that.
  • The "What can we automate?" tool runs in your browser. Your picks aren't sent anywhere unless you send the contact form.

How long we keep it

We keep messages and contact details for as long as we need them to answer you and for our business records, and then delete them. If you become a client, we keep the records our agreement or the law requires.

Your choices

Email hello@embroseai.com to ask what information we have about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted. We'll do it unless we're required to keep something, for example for tax records. Copies held by the services named above follow those services' own policies, and we'll point you to them if you ask.

Children

This site is meant for businesses and isn't directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll post the new version here and update the date at the top.
