Why real estate teams

A real estate team runs on follow-up. New leads need a reply in minutes, the CRM gets messy fast, and every deal carries dates that can't be missed. Most of that is computer work an AI bot can draft or track, while agents keep the relationships and the judgment calls. I'm a licensed REALTOR® myself, so I know what a team's day actually looks like.

What the Crew can handle for a team

  • Answer new portal leads fast. Drafts a personal reply within minutes, logs the lead in your CRM and texts the right agent a summary. The agent approves before the reply goes out unless you decide otherwise.
  • Keep the CRM clean. Merges duplicates, tags lead sources and flags contacts nobody has followed up with.
  • Listing descriptions and social posts. Turns agent notes and photos into draft descriptions and posts for the agent to review and edit.
  • Transaction checklists and deadlines. Watches the key dates on each deal and nudges the right person before anything slips.
  • Showing requests and scheduling. Collects requests, checks calendars and proposes times for approval.
  • AI on client documents without sending them out. A model running on your own computer, so documents stay in-house. See private local AI.

Example with sample data

Sample data, not a real client. A new lead comes in from a portal at 8:40 p.m. asking about a three-bedroom listing. The Crew bot drafts a reply that answers the question and offers two showing times from the listing agent's calendar, logs the lead in the CRM with its source, and texts the agent a two-line summary. The agent reads it, tweaks one sentence and taps approve.

What stays human

  • Agents approve client messages, offers and anything that touches a contract.
  • The bots work in accounts your team owns, with the least access needed, a full activity log and an off switch.
  • Your brokerage's rules and your CRM's settings stay in charge. We set the bots up to fit them.

Real estate FAQs

Can AI follow up with real estate leads automatically?

Yes. A Crew bot can draft a personal reply to a new portal lead within minutes, log it in your CRM and text the agent. At Embrose AI an agent approves the reply before it goes out, unless your team chooses otherwise.

Will it work with our CRM?

We confirm your CRM and tools on the intro call. The Crew works in the accounts you already use; I won't promise a connection I haven't checked.

Does it replace our transaction coordinator or ISA?

No. It takes repetitive tracking and drafting off their plate so they can spend time on people.

Do you work with teams outside Indianapolis?

Yes. In person within about an hour of Franklin, which covers Indianapolis, Greenwood and Johnson County, and remotely anywhere.

What does it cost to start?

The AI Audit is $1,500 fixed, and the 30-day Crew pilot is $1,500 for one bot on one workflow.
