AI for field, construction and home-services companies

The short answer

Embrose AI sets up AI bots for contractors, field crews and home-services companies (HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing, landscaping, utility and construction). The bots pull job details out of calls, texts and email, turn crew photos and voice notes into daily reports, propose schedules, draft quotes and invoices, and draft reminders on late payments. The office approves anything that goes to a customer. Collin Hill, an AI consultant based in Franklin, Indiana, sets it up in person around Greenwood, Indianapolis and Johnson County, or remotely. Why field businesses

The field work gets done. It's the office work around it that piles up: messages from five channels, reports written at night, schedules rebuilt every morning, quotes and invoices that wait until someone has time. I work as a Project Manager at TESCO and co-own a company that builds software for utility work, so my days are spent around projects, schedules and the paperwork that comes with them.

What the Crew can handle

Construction, utility and field crews

  • Job intake from calls, texts and email. Pulls job details out of messages into one clean work-order list.
  • Daily field reports. Turns crew photos and voice notes into a formatted daily report and flags open items.
  • Crew scheduling and dispatch. Checks crew calendars and proposes a schedule for the office to approve.
  • Estimate drafts. Drafts estimates from job notes and your price list for review.
  • AI on drawings and project files, kept in-house. A model on your own hardware. See private local AI.

Home services

  • Missed calls and web inquiries. Triages new inquiries and drafts a reply with your availability.
  • Quote drafts. Drafts quotes from the inquiry for the owner to approve.
  • Booking and appointment reminders. Proposes times, holds the slot and drafts reminder texts.
  • Review requests after the job. Drafts a thank-you and review request once a job is marked done.

Both

  • Invoice prep when a job closes, queued for approval.
  • Following up on late invoices, drafted for your approval.
  • Matching payments to invoices, with a person approving each one.

Example with sample data

Sample data, not a real client. A homeowner texts at 6:15 a.m.: "AC making a grinding noise, can someone come today?" The Crew bot adds the job to the work-order list with the address and issue, checks the day's schedule, and drafts a reply offering the 1–3 p.m. window. The office manager approves it from her phone before the first truck leaves.

What stays human

  • The office approves customer messages, quotes, credits and anything involving money.
  • Bots work in accounts you own, keep an activity log and can be paused any time.
  • This is back-office help, not accounting or tax work. It sits alongside whoever handles your books today.

Field and home-services FAQs

Can AI answer missed calls and web inquiries for a home-services business?

A Crew bot can triage new inquiries from your web form, email or texts and draft a reply with your availability. At Embrose AI the owner or office approves replies before they go out.

Can AI write daily field reports?

Yes. It can turn crew photos and voice notes into a formatted daily report and flag open items for the office to review.

Does it work with our field-service software?

We confirm your tools on the intro call. I build in the tools you already use and won't promise an integration I haven't checked.
