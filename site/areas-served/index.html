Why in person matters for this work

AI setup goes better when I can sit next to the person who does the work and watch how it's really done: which screens they open, what they copy where, what they check before they hit send. That's hard to see on a video call. So for businesses close to Franklin I'd rather come to you, at your office, shop or job trailer.

Where I meet clients in person

Franklin and Johnson County. This is home base. Franklin is the Johnson County seat, and I can usually meet the same week anywhere in the county, from Edinburgh and Trafalgar to Whiteland, New Whiteland and Bargersville.

Greenwood and Center Grove. Just up I-65 and US-31 from Franklin. Greenwood has a dense mix of the kinds of businesses I work with most: real estate teams, contractors and home-services companies, and offices of 5–50 people.

Indianapolis, especially the south side. Southport, Perry Township, Beech Grove and the rest of south Indy are a short drive. I also meet clients downtown and across Marion County.

Within about an hour of Franklin. That generally covers places like Columbus, Shelbyville, Martinsville and the surrounding counties. If you're not sure, call or text and ask.

Remote, anywhere. The intro call, audit interviews and most build work can happen over video.

How a first visit works

  1. Free 30-minute intro call by phone or video, or in person if you're close. We find the work that eats your team's time.
  2. If we do an AI Audit ($1,500), I come to your place of business, sit with the people who do the work and map it step by step.
  3. You get a written roadmap, and we go through it together in person or over video.
  4. If you build with me, setup and training happen on-site where it helps and remotely where it doesn't.
