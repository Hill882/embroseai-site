FAQ

What happens on the free intro call?

It's 30 minutes. We talk through how your business runs, find the work that eats your team's time, and decide whether an AI Audit, a bot pilot or something else makes sense. In person or by phone or video.

Do I need to be technical?

No. I handle the setup and train your team in plain English.

Who approves what the bots do?

Your team does. Bots propose sensitive actions such as customer messages, refunds, credits and write-offs, and a person approves them. Every bot keeps an activity log and can be paused at any time.

Do you meet in person?

Yes, anywhere within about an hour of Franklin, including Indianapolis, Greenwood, Johnson County and surrounding counties. Remote works too.

Is my data safe?

I scope access to the minimum needed, document what's connected, and keep you in control of accounts and data. If you want data to stay entirely in-house, ask about a private/local AI setup.

Can AI run on our own computers instead of the cloud?

Often, yes. With a private/local setup, the AI model runs on hardware you own, so your data stays in-house. It starts with the $1,500 AI Audit, where we scope hardware and setup, then I quote it. If you need new hardware, I can spec and build or source it, billed separately.

What does the retainer include?

Up to 10 hours a month of improvements, fixes and new automations for $1,500/mo, month to month, with a published cap so there are no surprises.

Are the hours in the automation map real?

No. They're illustrative examples for a typical small team, not promises or measured results. The AI Audit gives you real numbers for your business.

Where should I start?

Most businesses start with the AI Audit. Its roadmap tells you what's worth building. If billing work is the pain, the 30-day bot pilot is a good first step too.
